# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public landing page (`/`)
- Public authentication page (`/auth`)
- Public lead-generation tool pages (`/calculateur`, `/diagnostic`, `/rentabilite`)
- Shared public-facing acquisition UI linked from those pages

## Out of scope
- Authenticated application routes (`/dashboard`, `/customers`, `/orders`, `/services`, `/expenses`, `/payments`, `/reports`, `/machines`, `/employees`, `/analytics`, `/subscriptions`, `/settings`)
- Transactional invitation acceptance pages (`/join/:token`)
- User-specific shared report URLs (`/rapport/:leadId`) unless they reveal a sitewide technical SEO pattern that also affects in-scope public pages

## Target audience
- Laundry and dry-cleaning shop owners, managers, and operators
- Small and mid-sized laundries in Francophone, Anglophone, and Lusophone African markets

## Primary keywords
- laundry management software
- dry cleaning software
- pressing software
- laundry business software Africa
- laundry profitability calculator
- laundry startup cost calculator
