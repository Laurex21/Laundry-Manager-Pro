🧺 SMART ESTIMATION ENGINE

- 10kg machine → estimated detergent usage = 80–120g
- 10kg machine → estimated water consumption = 80–120L

📊 COST STRUCTURE & CALCULATION LOGIC
Calculate Direct Costs (water, electricity, detergent, labor), Indirect Costs (rent, maintenance, machine amortization, admin), and Operational Garment Costs (ironing, packaging, thermal sealing, perfume).
Formulas:
- Cost per gram detergent = (price detergent) / (total grams)
- Machine amortization = machine price / total lifetime cycles
- Cost per kg = total operational cost / total kg processed
- Recommended selling price = cost + desired margin
